01About

I started as the only engineer at FourM, an ad-tech startup, where I built the whole platform on my own. AnyMind Group acquired the company in 2017, and that system grew into AnyManager, an ad-delivery platform serving more than ten billion impressions a month.

Over the next nine years at AnyMind I went from tech lead to Head of Engineering to VP of Engineering, running teams across Japan, Thailand, Vietnam and India for a TSE-listed company. I care about functional, clean architecture on the backend, cloud cost that someone actually looks at, and engineering metrics that hold up in a performance review.

Since November 2026 I have been VP of Engineering at LR, Inc. I live in Bangkok.
